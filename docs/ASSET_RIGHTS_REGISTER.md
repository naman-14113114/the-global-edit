## Removed Assets

All inherited photographs, product packshots, competitor images, alleged expert portraits, testimonials, before-and-after images and the PDF remain removed because no rights records were available.

## Intake Rule

No new photograph, illustration, logo, screenshot, video or PDF may be published until this register records:

- File path.
- Creator or licensor.
- Source URL or delivery record.
- Licence or permission terms.
- Permitted territories, media and duration.
- Date checked.
- Any required attribution.

Original code-rendered illustrations should remain clearly illustrative and must not imply a real person, test, endorsement or result.
