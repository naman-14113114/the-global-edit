# The Global Edit Ads Compliance Baseline

Last reviewed: 12 August 2026

## Launch Position

The Global Edit is a non-commercial practical-living publication. Its pages exist to provide original, complete information, not to display ads, collect leads or redirect readers elsewhere.

The intended initial paid destination is:

`/guides/room-by-room-home-reset`

The campaign must describe that guide accurately and link directly to it without an intermediate redirect.

## Destination Rules

- Keep pages public, crawlable, reliable and usable on common desktop and mobile browsers.
- Keep the canonical hostname consistent: `https://www.theglobaledit.shop`.
- Provide substantial original content on the final URL.
- Keep navigation predictable and all visible links functional.
- Show the publication purpose, editorial policy, privacy notice and terms.
- Do not add pop-ups, interstitial gates, forced downloads, login walls or unexpected redirects.
- Do not show different subject matter to crawlers and people.

## Content Rules

- Practical home, travel-organisation and clothing-care information only.
- No ecommerce, affiliate links, product rankings, paid placement or commercial offers.
- No sensitive or regulated topics, personal-condition targeting or claims about likely outcomes.
- No fake experts, credentials, tests, reviews, testimonials, ratings or reader counts.
- No urgency, scarcity, prices, discounts, gifts, guarantees or sensational headlines.
- No third-party media without a recorded licence or permission.

## Tracking Rules

The site contains no Google Ads tag, Microsoft UET, analytics, remarketing pixel or other non-essential tracking. Before adding any such technology:

1. Review the current UK consent requirements.
2. Block non-essential storage and access until the required choice is made.
3. Record the choice and provide withdrawal.
4. Update the privacy and cookie notices.
5. Verify tags do not fire before the permitted state.

## Campaign Rules

- Start with UK English informational searches directly related to the final guide.
- Use a final URL on `www.theglobaledit.shop`, with no unrelated redirect.
- Keep the ad headline and description factual and present on the landing page.
- Do not use shopping formats, prices, promotions, countdowns, unsupported superlatives or sensitive-personalisation signals.
- Use a genuine advertiser name, billing country, payment method, verification record and domain ownership record.
- Do not create a new account to bypass a prior suspension or policy restriction.
