# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Adults in the United Kingdom who arrive from search because they want a clear, practical answer to an everyday home, clothing-care, or travel-organisation question. They may be reading quickly on a phone and should be able to use the guide without creating an account, sharing personal data, or buying anything.

## Product Purpose

The Global Edit is an independent practical-living publication. It publishes original, step-by-step guides and checklists that help readers organise ordinary tasks with less friction. Success means the advertised page directly answers the query, the publisher and editorial process are easy to identify, and the reader can leave with a useful method without being redirected to a seller.

The publication does not sell products, collect leads, publish affiliate links, provide regulated advice, or use health, financial, political, legal, gambling, adult, or other sensitive topics to attract traffic.

## Brand Personality

Calm, useful, transparent. The voice is specific and plain-spoken, with the confidence of a well-kept field guide. It avoids hype, urgency, inflated expertise, and promises about outcomes.

## Anti-references

- Advertorials disguised as independent editorial.
- Product-ranking, comparison, coupon, or review sites built mainly to send visitors elsewhere.
- Luxury-shopping language, fake scarcity, sensational headlines, testimonials, ratings, and invented experts.
- Generic AI landing pages with vague claims, repeated card grids, decorative gradients, or filler copy.
- Medical, cosmetic-treatment, financial, legal, political, or other regulated and sensitive subject matter.

## Design Principles

1. Answer the search intent on the page, near the top, with no gate or detour.
2. Publish original methods, examples, and checklists instead of repackaging other sites.
3. Make identity, dates, scope, and corrections visible wherever trust matters.
4. Prefer neutral instructions over recommendations, rankings, or promises.
5. Keep navigation predictable, pages fast, and the reading experience quiet.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Use semantic landmarks and headings, visible keyboard focus, sufficient contrast, large touch targets, reduced-motion support, descriptive link text, responsive layouts without horizontal scrolling, and plain English that does not assume specialist knowledge.
